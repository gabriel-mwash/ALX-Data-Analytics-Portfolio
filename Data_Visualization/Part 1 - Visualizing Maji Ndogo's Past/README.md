# 📊 Part 1: Visualising Maji Ndogo’s Past

## ❗ Problem Context

Maji Ndogo faces challenges in water access, with long queues at shared water points. There are concerns that women, who make up a large portion of these queues, may be disproportionately affected by crime.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyse gender composition at water queues
* Explore trends in crime data
* Identify relationships between queueing and crime incidents
* Build foundational visuals for future reporting

## 📊 Visualisations Created

* Line charts (trends over time)
* Scatter plots (relationship between variables)
* Composition charts (gender distribution)
* Custom maps (regional patterns using Shape Maps)
